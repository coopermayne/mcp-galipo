Privacy Policy — SMS/Text Messaging

The Law Offices of Dale K. Galipo ("we," "our," or "the Firm") respects your privacy. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information related to our text messaging communications with clients.

  • We collect client phone numbers and contact information solely for communication related to their legal matter.
  • Your information is not shared with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • All client data is kept confidential, consistent with attorney-client privilege and applicable professional responsibility rules.
  • Text messages may include case updates, appointment reminders, document requests, and scheduling communications.
  • You may request deletion of your phone number from our messaging system at any time by contacting our office.
